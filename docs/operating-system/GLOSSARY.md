# Glossary

## Project terms

- Ya-Fest: the public brand and creative platform.
- Producer Center "Ya": organizer identity used in existing site content.
- Creative platform: brand-level positioning for the ecosystem of festivals, campuses, workshops, laboratories, and educational programs.
- Creative campus: preferred product term for campus-style programs.
- Camp or "лагерь": search-language term allowed in FAQ/SEO explanation, not preferred brand-level positioning.
- Festival: event product with nominations, jury/experts, applications, and potential archive status.
- Laboratory: intensive learning or production format with a focused creative outcome.
- Workshop/intensive: shorter educational format led by a teacher or expert.
- Product map: normalized list of products with audience, dates, venue, price, inclusions, teachers, result, and evidence.
- GEO: Generative Engine Optimization; making the project understandable and quotable for AI answer systems through clear facts, public accessibility, proof, and structured data.
- llms.txt: proposed AI-readable site map/summary; useful but not a replacement for indexable pages.

## Status terms

- Implemented: exists in code/files and has relevant verification or explicit evidence.
- Skeleton: structure exists, but behavior or content is not complete.
- Source/evidence: material that can support work but is not proof of implementation.
- Current truth: current-state docs plus verified code/tests.
- Open: unresolved and not safe to assume.

## Deploy terms

- runtime-contract deploy: only runtime-contract image or contract surface was deployed.
- staging product deploy: a product staging surface was deployed and checked.
- production deploy: production release completed and was verified.

Do not call a partial deploy a deploy of the full product.
